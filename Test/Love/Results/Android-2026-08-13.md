## Transient-buffer regression

No game produced a `not enough transient buffer` diagnostic and no Dora process
exited during the 100-game run. Two focused checks were also run against the
same APK:

- Tiny Boatball ran for 300 frames; after LoveNode teardown,
  `Director.entry` contained one node and logcat contained zero transient-buffer
  warnings.
- `TransientBufferLifecycleScene` completed three create/run/destroy cycles of
  120 frames with 840 point draws per frame; logcat contained zero
  transient-buffer warnings.

This is an automated startup/render smoke test, not a claim that every gameplay
path, input method, audio path, or level was exercised. The non-pass diagnostics
identify the next compatibility backlog, including missing bundled modules or
assets, LuaJIT/legacy-Lua dependencies, and remaining API/signature differences.
