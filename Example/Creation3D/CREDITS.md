# Asset provenance

`cube.glb`, `sphere.glb`, `cylinder.glb`, and `plane.glb` are original unit assets generated by the adjacent `generate_assets.py`, under this repository's MIT license. The generator is offline, deterministic, and uses no third-party packages. Geometric counts: 12 / 224 / 64 / 2 triangles respectively.

`Fox.glb` is copied without modification from `Test/Model3D/Assets/Model/Fox.glb` in Dora-Example. It is a local animation fixture, not an AI-generated asset. Its embedded glTF attribution is retained verbatim below:

> CC-BY 4.0 Model by PixelMannen https://opengameart.org/content/fox-and-shiba and @tomkranis https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-fox-by-pixelmannen-animated-371dea88d7e04a76af5763f2a36866bc and @AsoboStudio with @scurest https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/pull/150#issuecomment-406300118

The copy is identical to the existing fixture; hashes and embedded metadata are recorded in `Test/Creation3D/evidence/assets.json`. No generated-service calls, uploads, rigging or retargeting are part of this sample.
